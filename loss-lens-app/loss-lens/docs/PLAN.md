# Loss Lens — 48-Hour Build Plan
**Global Energy Hackathon · Oct 30 – Nov 1, 2026 · Track: Digital Solutions, Apps & AI/ML Tools**

> **Loss Lens** is an AI copilot that helps distribution companies find where their electricity is being lost or stolen — and who is being unfairly overbilled — so honest customers stop paying for everyone else's losses.

---

## The pitch in one line
When a utility loses 25–30% of its energy to theft and faulty meters, honest customers pay for it in their tariff. Loss Lens lets a feeder manager ask in plain English where the losses are, and hands field crews a ranked, explained inspection list.

**Pillar:** Affordability (with Availability as a secondary benefit: less theft → less overload and load-shedding).

## Headline numbers (from the synthetic dataset — say so in the pitch)
- 30 feeders, 6,000 customers, 92 days of smart-meter reads
- Average feeder loss **13.8%**; five hook-theft feeders run at **27–32%**
- Hidden losses ≈ **607,000 kWh in 3 months ≈ PKR 33 million**
- Recovering them could cut honest customers' bills by about **4%**
- **72 customers overbilled** — Loss Lens finds 100% of them (the fairness story)
- Detection vs answer key: **5/5 hook feeders ranked top 5; 98.6% recall, 0 false alarms** on the inspection list

> Be honest with judges: rules perform this cleanly because the data is synthetic. Real AMI data is noisier — that's why the copilot shows its reasons and SQL, and why a human approves every inspection.

## Hours 2–10 · Core copilot
- [ ] Streamlit app skeleton: Ask box → SQL → result table → chart
- [ ] System prompt with the semantic layer: view descriptions, business rules ("loss % = (input − metered) / input", "excess loss = loss − expected technical loss"), 10–15 worked examples
- [ ] SQL validator: single read-only SELECT, allowlisted views only, row limit
- [ ] Self-correction: on SQL error, retry once with the error message

**Exit check:** "Which five feeders have the highest excess loss this month?" returns the right table and chart.

## Hours 10–16 · The three screens judges will remember
1. **Feeder balance** — ranked feeders by excess loss; click one → daily trend (input vs metered)
2. **Inspection list** — top suspects with risk score and plain-English reasons; filter by feeder; export as field work order (CSV)
3. **Fair billing** — overbilled customers with PKR amount to refund
